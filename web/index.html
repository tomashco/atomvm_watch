atomvm_watch

What is this?

atomvm_watch runs AtomVM apps, written in Elixir, Erlang or Gleam against atomvm_m5, on ESP32 watches. This page is both an emulator and an installer: the same .avm runs here in the browser (AtomVM compiled to WebAssembly) and on the watch.

  1. Try it: drop an .avm (for example from mix atomvm.packbeam) on the box below, or open one of the repo's examples. It starts in the emulator right away. Use the buttons under the watch or their keys (A, B, P for Power), and the battery slider. Output and errors show in the console. The page remembers the last app you loaded.
  2. Put it on the watch (Chrome or Edge, USB cable): open Install to device and click Connect. The first time on a watch, click Install runtime (the AtomVM firmware; the newest is selected, older ones are listed with what they lack). Then Install app writes only your .avm, in about a second, and restarts the watch.

Loading another app reloads the page, which closes the serial connection: click Connect again before installing it.
